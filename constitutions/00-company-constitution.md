# PromptProfit Company Constitution v1.0

**Status:** Ratified

---

# Mission

PromptProfit exists to help businesses convert more visitors into customers through trustworthy, intelligent, and measurable AI systems.

Every decision made within the company should support this mission.

---

# Vision

Build the world's most trusted AI Conversion Intelligence Platform.

PromptProfit should become the operating system that helps organizations understand visitor intent, make intelligent decisions, and improve commercial outcomes.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Customer Value First

Every feature must create measurable value for customers.

Technology is a means to that end, not the objective.

---

## 2. Truth Over Assumption

PromptProfit is driven by evidence.

Decisions should be based on real customer behaviour, measurable outcomes, and verified data rather than intuition alone.

---

## 3. Simplicity Scales

Prefer simple systems over clever systems.

Complexity must always justify its existence.

---

## 4. Long-Term Thinking

Build for durability.

Short-term speed must never create long-term architectural debt without deliberate justification.

---

## 5. Constitutional Engineering

The platform is governed by constitutions.

Architecture is intentional, documented, and consistent.

Engineering decisions should strengthen—not weaken—the platform.

---

## 6. AI as a First-Class Citizen

Artificial intelligence is a core capability of PromptProfit.

AI workers must operate within defined responsibilities, transparent decision-making, and human oversight where appropriate.

---

## 7. Security and Trust

Customer data is treated as a critical asset.

Security, privacy, and tenant isolation are foundational requirements—not optional enhancements.

---

## 8. Continuous Improvement

Every release should improve the platform.

Every experiment should increase understanding.

Every failure should increase knowledge.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

PromptProfit is engineered from the foundation upward.

Architecture precedes implementation.

The database precedes services.

Services precede interfaces.

Quality compounds over time.

---

# Decision Standard

When multiple approaches are possible, prefer the option that:

1. Preserves architectural consistency.
2. Improves maintainability.
3. Supports long-term scalability.
4. Delivers measurable customer value.

---

# Closing Principle

PromptProfit is not simply software.

It is a continuously improving intelligence platform built on disciplined engineering, measurable outcomes, and enduring architectural principles.
